What is an APS?
Your Admission Point Score turns your Grade 12 subject percentages into points. Institutions add them up and compare the total with the minimum for each programme. Each one calculates it a little differently, so always check the programme requirements.
Can I apply to more than one institution?
Yes. Most learners apply to several so they have a backup. Every institution has its own closing date and requirements, so check them before you start.
Where can I see closing dates?
Closing dates differ for each institution and programme. You’ll find them on our Deadlines page.
Is my personal information safe?
We only ask for what an application needs, and we keep it protected. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
