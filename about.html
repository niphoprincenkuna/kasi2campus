Who We Are

Applying to higher education can be overwhelming, especially while learners are still working to finish Grade 12 strong. We handle the application process on your behalf — from selecting institutions and courses, to submitting applications correctly and on time, to guiding you through NSFAS funding.

Our goal is simple: take the paperwork off your plate so you can put your energy where it matters most — your final exams.

Meet the Founder

Mr NP Nkuna

Founder, Kasi2Campus

Mr Nkuna founded Kasi2Campus with a passion for empowering young people and helping them unlock opportunities in higher education. Recognizing the challenges many learners face when applying to universities and colleges, he built a platform designed to provide clarity, guidance, and professional support throughout the application process.

Through Kasi2Campus, his aim is simple: every learner, regardless of background, should have access to the information and assistance needed to pursue their academic goals with confidence.
